Privacy policy

TLC+ Web is a free, open-source fan tool for designing tracks and exporting Gran Turismo 6 .ted files. You do not need an account. The editor has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or telemetry.

Your files and track data

Files you choose to import, including projects, TED files, images, GPX recordings and CSV paths, are processed in your browser. The editor does not upload their contents to the maintainer or a server. The current track, reference paths, undo history and imported images are held in memory for the open page. Closing or reloading it can discard unsaved work. Downloaded exports are saved by your browser to a location you choose.

“Save to Browser” stores a named project on this browser profile until you delete it or the browser clears its data. It is not a cloud backup. Names, coordinates and GPS-derived routes can reveal personal information: avoid private locations when sharing exports. Other people with access to your browser profile may access your saves. Local storage is not encrypted by TLC+ and is shared with pages on the same web origin.

Preferences and data minimisation

By default, preferences apply to the current page only. If you choose “Remember my settings”, the editor may persist theme, accent colour, language, panel visibility and size, and performance preferences. This choice is optional, can be withdrawn below, and is not needed to edit or export tracks. The editor requests no name, email, location permission or account information to work.

GitHub Pages hosting and external services

Your browser connects to GitHub Pages to download this website and its locally hosted assets. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. The maintainer does not receive those security logs through the editor. GitHub describes its own processing, recipients, retention, international transfers and privacy controls in its Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection. This notice does not promise a retention period for GitHub’s logs.

No third-party fonts, embedded videos, remote analytics scripts or advertising services are loaded by the editor. Following an external link, opening a GitHub issue or emailing the maintainer uses that provider’s service and privacy terms. Issue reports are public: do not include personal information in them.

The maintainer receives the contact details, message and any attachments you voluntarily send. They are used to answer your request and resolve support, privacy, accessibility or rights issues, on the basis of legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); where a specific legal duty requires processing, Article 6(1)(c) applies. Sending a message is voluntary. Please provide only what is needed for the request.

Email is handled using Gmail, so Google processes correspondence under its Privacy Policy, which explains recipients and international processing. Routine correspondence is intended to be deleted within 12 months after resolution. Records needed for a legal obligation or an actual dispute may be retained for the applicable obligation or claim period, with access limited to what is needed. The maintainer does not sell messages, use them for marketing or share them publicly without a separate basis.

Your choices and rights

You can use the editor without remembered settings, withdraw that choice, delete browser saves, and keep your own downloaded backups. Browser-only track data cannot be recovered remotely by the maintainer. Where the maintainer processes personal data about you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing based on legitimate interests; portability applies where its legal conditions are met. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Requests can be sent to the contact address above. You may complain to Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) or your competent supervisory authority. The editor does not profile users or make decisions about them with legal or similarly significant effects.

Manage storage

You can remove individual tracks using File → Load from Browser → Delete. To remove every browser save, download backups first, then use the button below. These controls affect this browser profile and origin, not other devices, downloaded exports, email or GitHub’s records.

Blocking browser storage still allows editing and file downloads, but remembered settings and browser saves may not work. You can also use your browser’s site-data settings. We will describe new storage purposes and obtain a new choice where required before enabling them.

Accessibility

We aim to improve access using WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a reference. This is a description of current support and known limitations, not a claim of full conformance or certification.

  • Keyboard focus is visible. Sidebar tabs support arrow keys, Home and End; menus support arrow keys and Escape.
  • Dialogs have names, keep focus inside while open, close with Escape and return focus when closed.
  • Controls have text labels or accessible names. Decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Use Edit → Track points to inspect, add, select, edit or delete track points using form controls without dragging on the canvas.
  • Use the numeric inputs beside sliders for precise values. Help contains desktop and mobile control instructions.
  • Reduced-motion preferences disable interface animation and the flashing warning effect.

Known limitations

The track, terrain, elevation and isometric views are visual canvas renderings. They do not yet have complete screen-reader equivalents. Decoration placement, rotation/scaling tools and some visual interactions still need more keyboard and non-drag alternatives. Not all accent colours, themes or canvas annotations have been verified for contrast. Full assistive-technology testing is still needed.

Report an accessibility barrier to the contact above. Tell us which task failed and, if useful, your browser and assistive technology. You do not need to disclose a disability or medical information.
